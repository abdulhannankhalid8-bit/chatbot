Acme Robotics — Internal Knowledge Base (Sample)

Company overview
Acme Robotics was founded in 2019 in Austin, Texas. We build collaborative robot arms for small manufacturers. Our flagship product is the AR-7 cobot, released in 2023.

Products
- AR-7: 7 kg payload, 900 mm reach, price starts at $18,500.
- AR-3: smaller arm for education, payload 3 kg, price $6,900.
- VisionKit: optional camera module for AR-7, adds $2,200.

Support hours
Technical support is available Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 Central Time.
Emergency hardware support for enterprise customers is 24/7 via +1-555-0100.

Password and account

Return policy
Hardware can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Software licenses are non-refundable after activation.

2024 revenue note
Preliminary internal figure: $42 million revenue in FY2024, up from $28 million in FY2023.
